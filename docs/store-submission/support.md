## English

CentyChat is a corporate messenger. Access and accounts are managed by [ORGANIZATION/ADMINISTRATOR]. For account access, disabled accounts, or a forgotten password, contact [CORPORATE IT CONTACT]. For an app problem, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] or visit [SUPPORT URL]. Include the app version, platform, approximate time, and a description of the problem; **do not send your password, session token, or confidential message contents** in a support request unless your organization's approved process requires it.

If messages do not appear, check connectivity and reopen the app to synchronize. Notifications require permission and, for remote push, the owner's FCM/APNs configuration; they may be unavailable in a given deployment. iOS calls do not currently wake a closed app through PushKit/CallKit.

To request in-app account deletion, sign in and open **Profile → Delete account**; enter your password and confirm. The server de-identifies the account and keeps previously sent messages under “Deleted employee.” For privacy requests beyond this function, contact [DATA CONTACT EMAIL OR URL].
